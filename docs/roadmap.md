# FootyMinds feature roadmap
| # | Release | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Top 5 leagues** | this plan (below), plus refresh-failure alerts |
| 2 | **Scorelines** | goals model (Dixon-Coles): most likely score, both teams to score, over 2.5 goals; better goal-difference tiebreaks in the simulator |
| 3 | **Profile pages** | player profile (becomes the search destination) and team page (form, xG trend, fixtures, squad values, season odds) |
| 4 | **Smarter predictions** | injury-aware team strength ("Palmer out: −6%"), predicted line-ups, in-season retraining, separate goalkeeper value model |
| 5 | **FPL upgrades** | multi-transfer planner with −4 hits, chip advisor, best XI under £100m, mini-league view, price-change predictions |
| 6 | **New pages** | gameweek review (hits, misses, upsets, xG over/under-performers); transfer-window mode |
| 7 | **Sharing** | shareable image cards, share buttons, simple usage analytics |
| 8 | **Engagement** | fans-vs-the-model leaderboard; weekly email/post (needs an account/database decision at that point) |
| 9 | **Housekeeping** | automatic season rollover, open-sourcing, fresher market-value source |
| 10 | **Redesign** | the "Matchday Dark" plan (appendix); hosting, no-sleep and custom-domain decisions happen here |
